Part toggles: the whole card is the switch

The picked design: C, the whole card is the toggle, with B's switch where C had a bare "ON". Click anywhere on a card, or Tab to its switch and press Space. The router card's "How it works" link stays a link and does not flip the part. Unchecking a part dims its art and states what the road loses; the game reads the switches when a run starts, never mid-race. attest needs gate.
